# Ohlone Giving: Research Brief

Compiled 2026-07-31 (lane/design). Purpose: ground Tend's browse → understand → donate
surfaces in each organization's own published words, and fence off claims the repo
cannot prove. Every factual claim carries a source. Where sources conflict with each
other, that conflict is itself a finding. Tend never arbitrates.

Tend currently hosts two programs in `lib/tribes.ts`: the Association of Ramaytush
Ohlone (Yunakin Land Tax) and the Muwekma Ohlone Preservation Foundation. Sogorea
Te' Land Trust is referenced but not hosted. All three are documented here because
their territories, vocabulary, and program names overlap and contradict one another.

---

## 1. Association of Ramaytush Ohlone (ARO): Yunakin Land Tax

### Identity and leadership

- The Association of Ramaytush Ohlone is a nonprofit that "represents the interests
  of the Ramaytush Tribe, the original peoples of the San Francisco Peninsula."
  Source: https://www.ramaytush.org/about-the-aro.html
- Dr. Jonathan Cordero is Founder and Executive Director of the ARO and Chair
  ("Metush") of the Ramaytush Tribe. Gregg Castro (t'rowt'raahl Salinan /
  rumsien-Ramaytush Ohlone) is Culture Director, not the executive head.
  Sources: https://www.ramaytush.org/ , https://www.flysfo.com/about/land-acknowledgement ,
  https://californiaican.org/about/gregg-castro/
- The Ramaytush Tribe is not federally recognized; Tend must never imply otherwise.

### Territory: their own published definition

- The Yunakin Land Tax applies to residents of "San Francisco County, in San Mateo
  County, and the portion of Santa Clara County north of San Antonio and Moody
  Roads." Source: https://www.ramaytush.org/donate.html
- The territory page gives an explicit southern boundary: from San Antonio Road /
  El Monte Ave / Moody Road on the Bay side (near the Palo Alto–Mountain View line)
  over the Santa Cruz Mountains to Pigeon Point on the ocean side.
  Source: https://www.ramaytush.org/ramaytush-territory.html
- ARO asserts exclusive sovereignty: "we do not share sovereignty in our lands with
  any other sovereign entity, including other Native tribes."
  Source: https://www.ramaytush.org/ramaytush-territory.html
  (Other tribes' published definitions disagree; see §3. Tend presents parallel
  claims, never a merged map. This assertion is ARO's own position, sourced here
  for context. It must not appear in Tend UI as a territorial fact.)

### The Yunakin Land Tax

- "Yunakin is the Ramaytush word for village. It is used here to convey our
  commonality as a community of people who live and work together in the ancestral
  homeland of the Ramaytush Ohlone peoples." Source: https://www.ramaytush.org/donate.html
- Framing: "a financial contribution made by residents who live along the San
  Francisco Peninsula… You can make a one time or recurring contribution."
  Source: https://www.ramaytush.org/donate.html
- Third parties (UCSF, CIIS) describe it as a voluntary contribution.
  Sources: https://opportunity.ucsf.edu/initiatives/ucsf-land-acknowledgment ,
  https://www.ciis.edu/land-acknowledgement
- **No rate, percentage, calculator, or suggested amounts are published anywhere on
  ramaytush.org.** Any suggested Yunakin amounts in Tend's UI are Tend's own
  suggestion and must be labeled as such, never implied to be ARO's guidance.

### Donation mechanics (verified)

- Online donations via Kindful (a Bloomerang platform): the donate buttons on
  ramaytush.org link to ramaytush.kindful.com. Source: links on
  https://www.ramaytush.org/donate.html
- Checks payable to "Association of Ramaytush Ohlone," 1004B O'Reilly Ave Ste 1B3,
  San Francisco, CA 94129. Source: https://www.ramaytush.org/donate.html
- ARO is a 501(c)(3), EIN 86-3991594, donations tax-deductible.
  Sources: https://www.ramaytush.org/donate.html ,
  https://projects.propublica.org/nonprofits/organizations/863991594

### Trust signals

- SFO installed a permanent Ramaytush Ohlone land acknowledgment (Nov 2022).
  Source: https://www.flysfo.com/about/land-acknowledgement
- Institutional partnerships: Exploratorium (annual California Native Homeland
  Festival), California Academy of Sciences, Filoli (Lamchin Interpretive Center),
  Sutro Stewards. Sources: https://www.ramaytush.org/ ,
  https://www.exploratorium.edu/visit/calendar/california-native-homeland-festival-2026
- Contributions fund: rematriation of ancestral homeland, revitalization of sacred
  traditions, research/consultation/education, ecological restoration, community
  garden, arts and culture projects. Source: https://www.ramaytush.org/donate.html

## 2. Muwekma Ohlone Tribe / Muwekma Ohlone Preservation Foundation

### Organizational structure

- Three entities: the Muwekma Ohlone Tribal Council (governing body), Muwekma Ohlone
  Tribe Inc. (501(c)(3), founded 2017), and the Muwekma Ohlone Preservation
  Foundation (MOPF, 501(c)(3), EIN 88-0910186). Tend's hosted program points at the
  Foundation. Sources: https://muwekmafoundation.org/tribal-entities ,
  https://muwekmafoundation.org/culture
- Enrollment: "for over 600 members."
  Source: https://www.muwekmafoundation.org/cultural-revitalization

### Territory: their own published definition

- "Our ancestral territory encompasses modern-day San Francisco, San Mateo, most of
  Santa Clara, Alameda, and Contra Costa counties, as well as portions of Napa,
  Santa Cruz, Solano, and San Joaquin counties."
  Source: https://www.muwekmafoundation.org/the-tribe
- This is a far broader claim than ARO's, and directly overlaps it (both claim parts
  of San Francisco, San Mateo, and Santa Clara counties). These claims are
  contested between the two organizations; Tend shows both, decides neither.

### Federal recognition status

- Muwekma descend from the Verona Band of Alameda County, federally acknowledged
  1914–1927, then dropped after a 1927 BIA field report with no act of Congress
  terminating the relationship. Sources: https://www.muwekmafoundation.org/federal-recognition ,
  https://www.muwekma.org/recognition-process.html
- Petition #111 filed January 1995; denied September 6, 2002; the D.C. Circuit
  affirmed the denial on March 1, 2013. Sources: https://ictnews.org/archive/muwekma-ohlone-denied-recognition/ ,
  https://www.muwekmafoundation.org/federal-recognition
- A federal rule change to let denied tribes re-petition was delayed by the Trump
  administration in February and again in June 2025; its status is unresolved in
  available reporting. ⚠️ Earlier Tend copy in `lib/muwekma-content.ts` asserted
  a "January 2025 federal rule" with a re-petition window "until February 2030".
  That entry was corrected in this lane to match the KQED reporting above.
  Source: https://www.kqed.org/news/12026194/native-tribes-pushing-for-us-recognition-will-have-new-option-trump-keeps-it
- 2024 "Trail of Truth" cross-country journey for recognition.
  Source: https://unicornriot.ninja/2024/muwekma-ohlone-tribe-rides-across-the-nation-for-federal-recognition-in-trail-of-truth/

### Named contribution program: "Shuumi Land Tax" (verified, and a flashpoint)

- MOPF publishes its own page titled "Shuumi Land Tax": "a voluntary annual
  contribution that settler peoples living on indigenous land offer to support the
  work of the Muwekma Ohlone Preservation Foundation," where "'Shuumi' (meaning
  gift in our Chochenyo language)." Verified directly at
  https://www.muwekmafoundation.org/shuumi (fetched 2026-07-31).
- This collides with Sogorea Te' Land Trust's better-known Shuumi Land Tax (§3).
  Two different organizations use the same Chochenyo word for separate contribution
  programs. Any UI that says "Shuumi" must name which organization it belongs to.

### Donation mechanics (verified)

- MOPF donate page: preset amounts ($25/$50/$100/$250/custom) with monthly/annual
  options; footer states "501(c)(3) Non-Profit (EIN: 88-0910186)" and "Donations
  are tax-deductible as allowed by law." Address: 2570 N. First St, 2nd Floor,
  San Jose, CA 95131. Source: https://www.muwekmafoundation.org/donate
- The tribe's own site (muwekma.org) uses a PayPal Donate button.
  Source: link on https://www.muwekma.org/
- No published rate schedule or calculator for the Muwekma Shuumi was found.
- Chochenyo language revitalization is led by Language Revitalization Chair
  Bernadette Quiroz. Source: https://www.muwekmafoundation.org/cultural-revitalization

## 3. Sogorea Te' Land Trust (STLT): Shuumi Land Tax

### Identity

- "An urban Indigenous women-led land trust that facilitates the return of
  Indigenous land to Indigenous people," based in the East Bay. Founded 2015 by
  Corrina Gould and Johnella LaRose, emerging from the 2011 109-day spiritual
  encampment at Sogorea Te' (Glen Cove, Vallejo). Sources:
  https://sogoreate-landtrust.org/about/ , https://sogoreate-landtrust.org/history/
- Corrina Gould is Tribal Chair/Spokesperson of the Confederated Villages of Lisjan.
  Source: https://sogoreate-landtrust.org/lisjan-history-and-territory/

### Territory: their own published definition

- "Our territory includes 5 Bay Area counties: Alameda, Contra Costa, Solano, Napa
  and San Joaquin." The Lisjan are "made up of the seven nations that were directly
  enslaved at Mission San Jose… and Mission Dolores," and are "directly tied to the
  'Indian Town' census of the 1920's and the Verona Band."
  Source: https://sogoreate-landtrust.org/lisjan-history-and-territory/
- The Shuumi city list covers Alameda/Contra Costa cities plus Benicia and Vallejo
  (Solano); it does not list San Francisco, San Mateo, or Santa Clara cities.
  Source: https://sogoreate-landtrust.org/shuumi-land-tax/
- ⚠️ Note what this means for Tend: for Alameda and Contra Costa counties, both
  Muwekma's and Lisjan/Sogorea Te's published definitions apply. And **two
  organizations (Muwekma and Confederated Villages of Lisjan) both claim Verona
  Band descent**. Never present the Verona Band as exclusive to one.

### Shuumi framing: use their exact words

- "Shuumi means 'gift' in the Chochenyo language… It is a voluntary annual
  contribution paid to the Sogorea Te' Land Trust by non-Native people who live on
  unceded Lisjan Ohlone land." Source: https://sogoreate-landtrust.org/shuumi-land-tax-faqs/
- "The Shuumi Land Tax is not a donation. It is a financial contribution that
  recognizes and respects the sovereignty of Native Nations… However, it is
  tax-deductible." Source: https://sogoreate-landtrust.org/shuumi-land-tax-faqs/
- Inspired by the Wiyot Nation Honor Tax; debuted 2015.
  Source: https://sogoreate-landtrust.org/shuumi-land-tax-faqs/

### Published rate guidance (STLT is the only org of the three that publishes rates)

- Renters: 0.5% of annual rent (rent ≤ $1,500/mo), 0.625% ($1,501–2,500), 0.75%
  ($2,501–3,500), 1% (≥ $3,501/mo). Homeowners: flat tiers by bedrooms. Studio
  $150/yr, 1BR $200, 2BR $300, 3BR $400, 4+BR $500. Institutional schedules exist
  for nonprofits and for-profits. All figures framed as "a recommendation, an
  invitation." Source: https://sogoreate-landtrust.org/shuumi-land-tax-faqs/
- These rates belong to STLT's Shuumi only. **They must never be presented as
  Yunakin or Muwekma guidance.**

### Donation mechanics (verified)

- Payment page embeds Salsa Labs donation widgets; one-time or recurring
  (monthly/annual). Checks to "Sogorea Te' Land Trust," PO Box 6758, Oakland, CA
  94603, memo "Shuumi Land Tax."
  Source: https://sogoreate-landtrust.org/pay-the-shuumi-land-tax/
- STLT is an independent 501(c)(3), EIN 82-4415931; contributions tax-deductible.
  Sources: https://sogoreate-landtrust.org/shuumi-land-tax-faqs/ ,
  https://www.influencewatch.org/non-profit/sogorea-te-land-trust-2/

### Trust signals

- Land sites include Lisjan (East Oakland), Rammay (West Oakland), Rinihmu
  Pulte'irekne (Oakland Hills), 'Ookwe (Richmond), and the West Berkeley Shellmound,
  purchased for $27M in 2024 and transferred to STLT to hold on behalf of the
  Confederated Villages of Lisjan. Sources: https://sogoreate-landtrust.org/about/ ,
  https://sogoreate-landtrust.org/lisjan-history-and-territory/
- ~$54M in assets as of 2026 reporting; "one of the wealthiest Indigenous land
  trusts in the nation." Source: https://www.berkeleyside.org/2026/02/18/sogorea-te-native-american-land-trust-fundraising-success
- The original Sogorea Te' village site is at Glen Cove, Vallejo (the 2011
  occupation). Rinihmu Pulte'irekne is a separate site in the Oakland Hills.
  Do not merge them. Sources: https://sogoreate-landtrust.org/history/ ,
  https://sogoreate-landtrust.org/about/

## 4. Terminology: each org's own vocabulary

- **ARO** self-names: "Ramaytush Ohlone," "original peoples of the San Francisco
  Peninsula," "Yelamu" for the San Francisco County people.
  Source: https://www.ramaytush.org/terminology.html
- **Muwekma** self-names: "Muwekma Ohlone Tribe of the San Francisco Bay Area,"
  heirs of the "Verona Band of Alameda County."
  Source: https://muwekmafoundation.org/tribal-entities
- **Sogorea Te'** self-names: "Lisjan," "Lisjan (Ohlone)," "Confederated Villages
  of Lisjan". "Ohlone" appears only parenthetically.
  Source: https://sogoreate-landtrust.org/lisjan-history-and-territory/
- The word "Ohlone" itself is contested. ARO's terminology page says it resulted
  from a misspelling of "Oljon," and states: "Inaccurate terminology, like the use
  of Muwekma Ohlone to identify the original peoples of the San Francisco Bay Area,
  still linger[s] in various public venues and documents." Some groups (Amah
  Mutsun, Tamien Nation) decline "Ohlone" entirely.
  Source: https://www.ramaytush.org/terminology.html
- Practical rule for Tend: name each organization exactly as it names itself, and
  never use one org's vocabulary to describe another's people or land.

## 5. Do-not-conflate rules (binding for all Tend copy and UI)

1. **Yunakin is not Shuumi.** Yunakin ("village," Ramaytush language) is ARO's
   program. Shuumi ("gift," Chochenyo) belongs to Sogorea Te' and, separately, to
   Muwekma's foundation (rule 2). Different programs, geographies, and recipients.
2. **Two orgs publish a "Shuumi Land Tax."** Sogorea Te' Land Trust AND the Muwekma
   Ohlone Preservation Foundation both use the name. Never say "Shuumi" without
   naming the organization. Verified: https://sogoreate-landtrust.org/shuumi-land-tax/
   and https://www.muwekmafoundation.org/shuumi
3. **Geography is contested.** ARO claims the SF Peninsula plus NW Santa Clara
   (north of San Antonio/Moody Roads); Muwekma claims San Francisco, San Mateo,
   most of Santa Clara, Alameda, Contra Costa, and parts of four more counties;
   Lisjan/Sogorea Te' claims Alameda, Contra Costa, Solano, Napa, San Joaquin.
   These overlap and contradict. Present overlapping claims as overlapping claims.
   Never name one tribe as the sole people of a place. Where Tend shows Ramaytush
   for Santa Clara County, the scope is NW Santa Clara only, not the whole county.
4. **Verona Band descent is claimed by two orgs** (Muwekma and Confederated Villages
   of Lisjan). Never treat it as exclusive.
5. **Self-naming is not interchangeable.** "Ramaytush Ohlone" (ARO), "Muwekma
   Ohlone" (Muwekma), "Lisjan (Ohlone)" (Sogorea Te'). ARO explicitly rejects
   "Muwekma Ohlone" as a label for Peninsula peoples.
6. **Federal recognition stances differ.** No Ohlone tribe is federally recognized.
   Muwekma was previously recognized (1914–1927, as the Verona Band) and is
   actively seeking reaffirmation; Sogorea Te'/Lisjan has never been recognized and
   publicly disavows needing it. Don't blur these stances.
7. **No implied partnership or endorsement.** Tend links to and describes these
   programs; nothing in the repo proves any org's approval of Tend. No "partner,"
   "official," "in collaboration with," or logo-borrowing language.
8. **Tax-deductibility attaches to the named nonprofit only.** ARO EIN 86-3991594;
   MOPF EIN 88-0910186; STLT EIN 82-4415931. The tribal governments themselves are
   not 501(c)(3)s. Never extend deductibility claims to them.
9. **Framing:** Sogorea Te' insists Shuumi is "not a donation." When Tend presents
   Shuumi, mirror "voluntary annual contribution." "Donation/contribution" language
   is acceptable for ARO and MOPF general giving.
10. **Payment rails are verified per org:** ARO → Kindful + check; MOPF →
    Squarespace donation form; muwekma.org → PayPal; STLT → Salsa Labs + check.
    Tend's own Stripe rails are Tend's. Never imply the orgs receive funds through
    Tend unless a tribe has actually connected an account.
11. **No invented rates.** Only STLT publishes a rate schedule. Suggested amounts
    Tend shows for Yunakin or Muwekma giving are Tend's own suggestion and must be
    labeled as such.
12. **Demonstration honesty.** Tend is a hackathon prototype. Demo/test payments,
    testnet receipts, and unprovisioned tribal Stripe accounts must be labeled as
    what they are. Never claim live fundraising, real beneficiary onboarding, or
    partnership that the repo cannot prove.
